## Step 1 — Analyze the Reference Video
Video Analysis Instruction:
Analyze the uploaded reference video frame by frame. Identify and break down the complete visual structure, including:
- Total video duration and exact timing of every scene/change
- Number of shots, transitions, and key animation moments.
- Camera movement: zoom in/out, pan, tilt, rotation, tracking, parallax movement, and speed changes.
- Object movement: position changes, scale animation, rotation, reveal timing, and exit movements.
- Motion design style: easing, acceleration, deceleration, bounce, smoothness, and motion blur behavior.
- Layer structure: background, foreground objects, text layers, UI elements, particles, lighting effects, and depth elements.
- Composition: subject placement, spacing, alignment, camera angle, and visual hierarchy.
- Transition style between each shot and how elements connect visually.
- Lighting behavior, shadows, reflections, glow effects, and atmosphere.
- Color palette, contrast level, saturation, and overall color grading style.

Create a detailed timeline breakdown:
- 0–X seconds: opening frame, camera movement, appearing elements, animation timing, visual effects.
- X–X seconds: next motion sequence, transition, changes.
- Final seconds: ending composition, final movement, exit animation.

After analyzing, provide: 1) Exact motion recreation plan 2) Camera movement instructions 3) Animation/keyframe instructions 4) Transition instructions 5) Sound design suggestions 6) A redesigned version with the same motion but a completely different visual style and color grade.

Preserve the original motion language, timing, and pacing. Only change the visual identity, graphics, materials, and color treatment.

## Step 2 — Define a New Visual Direction
Keep: timing, camera path, animation rhythm, transition structure.
Change: colors, materials, graphic style, lighting design.
Example: premium futuristic brand film with glass surfaces, metallic materials, clean studio lighting, modern editorial design.

## Step 3 — Build New Visual Assets
Background: clean studio environment, soft gradient depth, floating particles, natural shadows.
Objects: glass panels, metallic elements, abstract 3D forms, modern UI-inspired components.
Effects: subtle glow, realistic reflections, depth of field, natural motion blur.

## Step 4 — Generation Prompt Structure
- Reference Motion: follow the exact motion choreography from the reference video.
- Camera: maintain identical framing, movement speed, zoom, rotation, transitions.
- Animation: preserve reveal timing, easing curves, movement rhythm.
- New Visual Style: premium cinematic design language, realistic materials, elegant lighting, modern visual elements.
- Quality: smooth motion, realistic physics, clean transitions, no glitches, no morphing, no AI artifacts.

## Step 5 — Motion Refinement
Camera: movement matching? speed correct? Animation: reveals at same moments? transitions smooth? Visual: feels like a new design? premium quality?

## Step 6 — Natural Cinematic Color Grade
Direction: balanced contrast, natural skin/material tones, soft highlights, detailed shadows, controlled saturation, cinematic depth.
Avoid: over-saturated colors, artificial neon, heavy filters, fake AI glow.

## Master Edit Prompt
Recreate the exact motion structure from the reference video. Keep the same pacing, camera movement, zoom speed, object placement, transitions, and overall animation flow. Do not change the timing or motion choreography.

Replace the original visual design with a new premium futuristic creative style. Transform the background into a clean dark graphite studio environment with subtle 3D depth layers, floating abstract shapes, soft light gradients, and modern editorial design elements. Replace the original graphic elements with sleek glassmorphism panels, smooth metallic surfaces, and minimal UI-inspired animations.

Maintain all text/object reveal timing exactly like the reference, but redesign every element with a fresh visual language. Use smoother kinetic motion, elegant scale animations, soft rotations, and cinematic parallax depth.

Color grade: premium cinematic palette with deep charcoal shadows, cool blue highlights, soft silver tones, controlled contrast. Subtle bloom, realistic reflections, soft shadows, high-end commercial lighting. Clean, sharp, modern — no oversaturation.

Motion style: ultra-smooth camera moves, professional motion design, natural easing curves, realistic motion blur, seamless transitions, no sudden jumps. Preserve the exact animation energy of the reference while making it feel like a new premium brand film.

Output: cinematic 4K quality, clean details, realistic lighting, polished motion graphics, no glitches, no distortion, no AI artifacts.
